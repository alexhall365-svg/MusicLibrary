# Shared groups: a guide for directors and members

A shared group is a library of music and lists that one person (the director) shares with a choir, band, orchestra or any ensemble.

## For the director (whoever runs the group's music)
- **Create it:** open **iCloud & Groups** (sidebar → Tools) → **Create Shared Group**. Hosting needs Pro.
- **Add music:** touch and hold any score → **Add to *your group***. Or add your own scores to a group list: they join the group automatically. New imports always go into your own library first.
- **Make lists:** in the group's sidebar section, tap **New Group List**, e.g. "Evensong – Advent Sunday" or "Spring Concert". Add scores and drag them into order. Everyone sees the same order.
- **Mark up the music for everyone:** annotations you draw on group music are the **director's markings**. Every member sees them, under their own markings. Breath marks, bowings, cuts and cues only need drawing once.
- **Invite people:** **iCloud & Groups** → **Share Link and Manage People** → **Copy Link**, then send it by Messages, WhatsApp or email. Anyone with the link can join and view. Only you can change the music, lists and director's markings.
- **Remove music:** touch and hold a group score → **Remove from Group…**. It's removed from every member's devices. As the owner you can keep your own copy.
- **Storage:** the group's PDFs count against your iCloud storage (5 GB free). **iCloud & Groups** shows how much you use.

## For members
- **Join:** install the app, then tap the link the director sent. The group appears in your sidebar. Joining is free.
- **Rehearse offline:** every group PDF downloads in full and stays on your device, so it works without Wi-Fi.
- **The director's markings:** appear on the music automatically and update when the director changes them. You can't change them. To hide them, turn off **Show Director's Markings** in **iCloud & Groups**.
- **Your own markings are private:** anything you draw on group music syncs between your own iPad and iPhone, and nobody else sees it.
- **Your own order:** group lists are view only. Touch and hold a list → **Copy to My Setlists** to make a copy you can change.
- **Leaving:** **iCloud & Groups** → swipe the group → **Leave**. Its music leaves your devices; your own library isn't touched.
